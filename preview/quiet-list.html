          Gestures and shortcuts
          Add
          Pull the list down, tap +, or press N. Paste several lines to add them all.
          Done
          Swipe a task right, or tick the circle.
          Delete
          Swipe a task left, or open it and choose Delete. Ctrl/⌘+Z undoes.
          Reorder
          Hold and drag, drag the grip, or focus the grip and press ↑ ↓.
          Switch list
          Swipe sideways anywhere that is not a task, use the dots, or press [ ].
          On the Lists screen
          Swipe a list right to add to it without opening it (+ when focused). Swipe left to delete it, after a confirmation (Delete when focused).
