                Gestures and shortcuts
                Add
                Pull the list down, tap +, or press N. Paste several lines to add them all.
                Insert between
                Spread two fingers apart between two tasks, or open a task and choose Insert below (+ on a focused task).
                Done
                Swipe a task right, or tick the circle. Quick runs climb in pitch.
                Delete
                Swipe a task left, or open it and choose Delete. Ctrl/⌘+Z undoes; Recently deleted keeps it for 30 days.
                Reorder
                Hold and drag, drag the grip, or focus the grip and press ↑ ↓.
                Move
                Open a task and choose Move to send it to another list.
                Switch list
                Swipe sideways anywhere that is not a task, use the dots, or press [ ].
                New list
                On the Lists screen: pull down for one at the top, tap the empty space below for one at the end, spread two fingers between two lists for one in between, or use the New list button.
                List swipes
                Swipe a list right to add to it without opening it (+ when focused). Swipe left to delete it, after a confirmation (Delete when focused).
                Zoom
                Two-finger zoom is off over the rows so the spread gesture can insert there. It still works on the title area, your phone's own zoom still works, and Text size above makes everything larger.
